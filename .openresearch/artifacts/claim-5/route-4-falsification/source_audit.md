# Claim 5 route 4 source audit

The exact compound claim concerns three target-SVHN Table 2 comparisons and
says the printed mean and standard deviation are derived from ten runs. It is
a historical aggregate statement, not a universal claim that every seed or
reasonable reconstruction yields those values.

A valid falsification must use the same complete protocol, all three
architectures, and independently recompute the six summaries from raw
predictions. The current official repository has no digit pipeline. The
predecessor omits `ECLoss_hd`, uses settings conflicting with Appendix J, and
has no seed schedule, checkpoints, or raw ten-run observations. The paper
omits the digit ECL cross-entropy coefficient and standard-deviation
convention.

Route 4 therefore checks for an internal arithmetic impossibility and
adjudicates whether any prior route meets every counterexample assumption. A
failed run, a repaired implementation, a different single seed, or missing
architectures is explicitly rejected as falsification.
