# Claim 2 route B method

The stress route uses the official simplex-anchor and temperature rules on
finite, exactly enumerable three-class covariate-shift populations. It scans
well-conditioned, many-bin, opposing-corner-mass, and diffuse opposing-mass
regimes over four sample sizes and 64 deterministic replicates per setting.

The verifier records every soft count, weight, empirical loss, exact
population loss, and displayed radius. A separate standard-library checker
recomputes every radius and error ratio without importing the experiment
implementation.
