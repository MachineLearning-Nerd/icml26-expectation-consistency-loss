corrected proof, runs 2,880 deterministic finite-support diagnostics over
`B in {2,4,8,16}`, `K in {2,3,10,50}`, and multiple sample sizes, and rejects
undefined-count and adaptive-function controls. A separate checker recomputes
the displayed radius and every error ratio without importing NumPy or the
certificate implementation.

The numerical sweep is diagnostic evidence. The general verification rests
on the explicit proof obligations documented in `source_audit.md`.
