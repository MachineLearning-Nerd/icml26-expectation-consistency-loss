# Claim 6 route 2 method

This route applies the paper's own Section 3.5 definition to its Table 1 ECL
row. It consumes the cumulative exact Claim 3 certificate, requires every
probability-domain and source-pin gate to pass, and carries four independent
contradictions into an explicit conjunction evaluation.

A second stdlib-only checker independently recomputes two exhaustive
finite-population expectations using `fractions.Fraction`. It imports neither
the primary Claim 3 certificate code nor the Claim 6 linkage code. Negative
controls preserve the boundary that a differently scaled, fixed-direction
mini-batch estimator can be unbiased.
