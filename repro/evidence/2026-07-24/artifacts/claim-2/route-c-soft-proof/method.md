# Claim 2 route C method

For each soft bin, the assignment is a number in `[0,1]` and all assignments
for one sample sum to one. A self-normalized vector concentration lemma bounds
the weighted conditional posterior mean by the inverse square root of its
realized soft count. Weighted Cauchy–Schwarz combines all bins. For target
counts, `sum_j w_hat_j / N_tj = B/N_t` exactly. A separate bounded-difference
argument controls empirical target bin masses and is absorbed by that same
term.

The conservative constant budget is `8*sqrt(2)+4 < 16`. The independent
checker recomputes the displayed radii, target-count identity, constant budget,
and all 1,024 adversarial soft-bin diagnostics without importing the proof
script.
